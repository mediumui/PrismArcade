About Quick Tap Grid

Quick Tap Grid is a free online reflex game that runs in the browser with no download or account. A random cell lights up in a 4x4 grid; tap it before it disappears to score. Each successful tap adds one point, and consecutive correct taps build your streak. Rounds last 30 seconds, so focus and speed are the key skills.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Quick Tap Grid on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Watch for glowing targets to appear on the grid.
  • 2. Tap each target before it disappears.
  • 3. Hit targets back-to-back to build a streak.
  • 4. Score as many points as you can in the 30-second round.
