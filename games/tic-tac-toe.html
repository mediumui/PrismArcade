Tic Tac Toe

A fast two-player logic match. Get three in a row to win. Best for quick breaks and casual strategy play.

Status Player XScore X 0Score O 0Draws 0

How to Play

Two players share the screen. Place your mark in any empty cell. The first player to line up three in a row wins.

  • Player X always goes first.
  • The match continues until a line is formed or the board fills.
  • Use New Round to reset only the board, keeping scores.
