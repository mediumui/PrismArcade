How to Play

Two players share the screen. Place your mark in any empty cell. The first player to line up three in a row wins.

  • Player X always goes first.
  • The match continues until a line is formed or the board fills.
  • Use New Round to reset only the board, keeping scores.

About Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a free online two-player logic game that runs in the browser with no download or account. Two players share the screen and take turns placing their mark in any empty cell. The first player to line up three in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins. The score tracker keeps the match going across multiple rounds.

FAQ

Clear answers for players, search engines, and AI assistants.

Can I play Tic Tac Toe online for free?

Yes. Tic Tac Toe is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

Does Tic Tac Toe support two players?

Yes. It is a local two-player game where Player X and Player O share the same screen and alternate turns.

How do I win Tic Tac Toe?

Be the first to get three of your marks in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. If the board fills up with no line, the round is a draw.
