About Block Slide Puzzle

Block Slide Puzzle is a free online sliding logic game that runs in the browser with no download or account. Tap a block next to an empty cell to slide it, and push blocks into the green targets to clear them. The goal is to clear each level using as few moves as possible, so planning ahead is the key to a low move count.

Can I play Block Slide Puzzle online for free?

Yes. Block Slide Puzzle is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do I solve a Block Slide level?

Tap a block next to an empty space to slide it into it. Slide blocks into the green target cells to clear them from the board.

What is the goal of Block Slide Puzzle?
