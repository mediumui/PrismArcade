About Easy Sudoku

Easy Sudoku is a free online 4x4 logic puzzle that runs in the browser with no download or account. Fill each empty cell with a number from 1 to 4 so that every row, column, and box contains each number exactly once. It is a fast, one-screen warm-up designed for players who want a short, satisfying logic session.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Easy Sudoku on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Fill the 4x4 grid with the numbers 1 to 4.
  • 2. Each row, column, and 2x2 box must contain 1 to 4 exactly once.
  • 3. Use the given clues to deduce the empty cells.
  • 4. Complete the grid with no repeats to finish.

FAQ

Common questions players ask.

Can I play Easy Sudoku online for free?

Yes. Easy Sudoku is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do I play Easy Sudoku?

Select an empty cell, then tap or press the corresponding number from 1 to 4. Use the Hint button if you get stuck, and New Puzzle to start a fresh board.

What makes Easy Sudoku different from standard Sudoku?

Easy Sudoku uses a 4x4 grid with numbers 1 to 4 instead of a 9x9 grid with 1 to 9, making it a shorter, faster puzzle ideal for beginners or quick breaks.
