About Bubble Pop

Bubble Pop is a free online bubble shooter that runs in the browser with no download or account. Aim the cannon at the bottom, fire a colored bubble, and connect three or more of the same color to pop them. Clear the whole board to win, and use wall bounces to reach tricky spots. It is a relaxing, one-screen puzzle built for short breaks.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Bubble Pop on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Aim the cannon with your mouse or finger — the dotted line shows your shot, including wall bounces.
  • 2. Click or tap to fire the loaded bubble toward the cluster above.
  • 3. Connect three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.
  • 4. Clear every bubble from the board to win; detached bubbles fall for bonus points.
