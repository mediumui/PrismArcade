About Hangman

Hangman is a free online word guessing game that runs in the browser with no download or account. Pick a category, guess letters to reveal the hidden word, and avoid the gallows. With Animals, Fruits, Countries and General word lists plus a hint button, it is a relaxing way to build vocabulary and spelling for players of any age.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Hangman on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Pick a word category such as Animals, Fruits, Countries, or General.
  • 2. Tap a letter or press a key to guess it.
  • 3. Correct letters fill in the hidden word; wrong letters add to the gallows.
  • 4. Reveal the whole word before six wrong guesses to win.

FAQ

Common questions players ask.

Can I play Hangman online for free?

Yes. Hangman is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do I play Hangman?

Choose a category, then tap or press a letter. Correct letters fill in the word; wrong letters draw another part of the gallows. Guess the word before six wrong letters.

What is the goal of Hangman?

Reveal the hidden word before the gallows is complete. Use the hint button if you get stuck, and your win count is saved on this device.
