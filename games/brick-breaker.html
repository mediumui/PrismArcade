Game Notes

Move the paddle with your mouse, finger, or the arrow keys. Click, tap, or press Space to launch the ball.

About Brick Breaker

Brick Breaker is a free online arcade game that runs in the browser with no download or account. Move the glowing paddle to keep the ball alive, bounce it into the brick wall, and clear every brick to win. Power-ups drop as you play, and your best score is saved on this device for instant replay.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Brick Breaker on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Move the paddle with your mouse, finger, or the left and right arrow keys.
  • 2. Click, tap, or press Space to launch the ball from the paddle.
  • 3. Bounce the ball into the brick wall to smash every brick.
  • 4. Catch the power-ups that drop and clear the board to win.
