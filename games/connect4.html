Game Rules

Red and Blue alternate turns. A disc falls to the lowest open row in the chosen column.

  • Win with four in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • Board full with no winner counts as a draw.
  • Use New Game to reset the board.

About Connect 4 Lite

Connect 4 Lite is a free online two-player board game that runs in the browser with no download or account. Red and Blue alternate turns dropping discs into a grid. The goal is to connect four discs in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally before your opponent does. It is a clean local two-player strategy match designed for quick breaks.

FAQ

Clear answers for players, search engines, and AI assistants.

Can I play Connect 4 online for free?

Yes. Connect 4 Lite is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

Does Connect 4 Lite support two players?

Yes. It is a local two-player game where Red and Blue share the same screen and take turns.

How do I win Connect 4 Lite?

Be the first to line up four of your discs in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. If the board fills up with no winner, the match is a draw.
