FAQ

Common questions players ask.

Can I take a typing speed test online for free?

Yes. The typing test is free, runs in the browser, and needs no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How is WPM calculated in this typing test?

WPM is your correctly typed characters divided by five, then divided by the minutes elapsed. Accuracy is the share of keystrokes that matched the passage.

How long does the typing test last?

The default run is 60 seconds and starts on your first keystroke. You can restart at any time for another attempt.
