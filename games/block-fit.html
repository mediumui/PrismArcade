About Block Fit

Block Fit is a free online block puzzle that runs in the browser with no download or account. Drag one of three shapes onto an 8x8 grid, pack the space carefully, and clear any full row or column for points. Clearing several lines in a single move raises a combo multiplier, and the run ends when no tray shape fits anywhere on the board.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Block Fit on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Drag a shape from the tray onto the 8x8 grid, or tap it then tap the board.
  • 2. Fit it into the open squares — shapes cannot be rotated.
  • 3. Fill a whole row or column to clear it and score bonus points.
  • 4. Clear several lines in one move to raise the combo multiplier.

FAQ

Common questions players ask.

Can I play this block puzzle online for free?

Yes. Block Fit is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do you score in Block Fit?

You score one point per square placed, plus ten points for every row or column you clear. Clearing more than one line in a move raises the combo multiplier.

When does a Block Fit run end?

The run ends when none of the three shapes in the tray can fit anywhere on the 8x8 grid.
