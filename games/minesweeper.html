Game Notes

Left-click a tile to reveal it. Each number tells you how many mines sit in the eight tiles around it. Right-click (or use Flag mode on mobile) to mark a suspected mine.

  • Reveal every safe tile to win.

About Minesweeper

Minesweeper is a free online logic puzzle that runs in the browser with no download or account. Left-click to reveal a tile and read the numbers that tell you how many mines sit nearby, and right-click to flag a suspected mine. Clear every safe tile to win, and your best time is saved on this device.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Minesweeper on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Left-click a tile to reveal it and read the number it shows.
  • 2. Each number tells you how many mines are hidden in the eight tiles around it.
  • 3. Right-click to flag a tile where you believe a mine is buried.
  • 4. Reveal every safe tile to win — never click a flagged mine.
