About Brain Quiz

Brain Quiz is a free online knowledge quiz that runs in the browser with no download or account. Each round presents five multiple-choice questions across mixed topics such as science, language, and general knowledge. Answer correctly to build your score; wrong answers show the correct option so you learn as you play.

Yes. Brain Quiz is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

Brain Quiz mixes general knowledge topics including science, language, technology, and everyday facts into each five-question round.
