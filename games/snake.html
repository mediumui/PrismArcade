About Neon Snake

Neon Snake is a free online reflex game that runs in the browser with no download or account. Use the arrow keys on desktop or the on-screen buttons on mobile to steer the snake and collect glowing energy nodes. Every point you collect grows the snake and slowly raises the pace. Hit a wall or your own body and the run ends, so calm focus is the key skill.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Neon Snake on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Use the arrow keys on desktop or the on-screen buttons on mobile to steer the snake.
  • 2. Collect the glowing energy nodes to grow longer and raise your score.
  • 3. Avoid running into the walls or into your own body.
  • 4. Survive as long as you can — the snake speeds up with every point you collect.
