How to Play

Every move slides all tiles. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one. Build your way up to the 2048 tile.

  • Arrow keys work on desktop.
  • The Swipe buttons work on touch.
  • Game ends when no moves are left.

About 2048

2048 is a free online merge puzzle that runs directly in the browser with no download or account. Slide tiles using arrow keys or on-screen swipe buttons; equal tiles merge into one. The goal is to reach the 2048 tile and rack up the highest score. Every move is instant, so sessions stay short and satisfying.
