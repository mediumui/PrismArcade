About Nonogram

Nonogram is a free online logic picture puzzle that runs in the browser with no download or account. The numbers beside each row and above each column tell you the lengths of the solid blocks of filled squares, with at least one empty square between blocks. Every puzzle generated here is verified by a solver to have exactly one solution, so it can always be finished with logic alone.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Nonogram on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Read the numbers beside each row — each one is the length of a solid block of filled squares.
  • 2. Click or drag to fill the squares those blocks must occupy.
  • 3. Switch to Mark mode to cross out squares you have proved are empty.
  • 4. Use Check at any time, and finish when every filled square matches the clues.

FAQ

Common questions players ask.

Can I play Nonogram online for free?

Yes. Nonogram is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do you solve a Nonogram?

Each number at the side of a row is the length of a solid block of filled squares in that row, and blocks are separated by at least one empty square. Combine the row and column clues to deduce which squares are filled.

Does this Nonogram have more than one solution?

No. Every puzzle generated here is checked by a solver so it has exactly one solution, which means it can always be finished by logic alone.
