About Whack-a-Dot

Whack-a-Dot is a free online reflex game that runs in the browser with no download or account. Dots pop up randomly in a 3x3 grid; tap or click each one before it disappears to score. The round lasts 30 seconds, so speed and accuracy are the key skills. Your best score is saved on this device for instant replay value.

FAQ

Clear answers for players, search engines, and AI assistants.

Can I play Whack-a-Dot online for free?

Yes. Whack-a-Dot is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do I play Whack-a-Dot?

Tap or click each dot as soon as it appears in the grid. Each successful hit scores one point before the 30-second timer ends.

What is the goal of Whack-a-Dot?

Score as many points as possible in a 30-second round by swatting the dots before they disappear.
