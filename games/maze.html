About Maze

Maze is a free online maze puzzle that runs in the browser with no download or account. Every board is a brand-new random labyrinth generated by a maze algorithm, so no two runs are alike. Move with arrow keys, WASD, a swipe on touch, or a click toward the exit, then reveal the shortest path with the Show Path button. Pick Small, Medium, or Large and try to beat your best time.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Maze on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Move the orange dot from the top-left corner toward the green exit.
  • 2. Use arrow keys or WASD on desktop, or swipe on mobile.
  • 3. Each maze is random, so find the route before you run out of time.
  • 4. Tap Show Path to reveal the shortest route to the exit.

FAQ

Common questions players ask.

Can I play Maze online for free?

Yes. Maze is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do I play Maze?

Move the orange dot from the top-left corner to the green exit. Use arrow keys or WASD on desktop, swipe on mobile, or tap toward a direction. The Show Path button reveals the shortest route.

Are the mazes the same every time?

No. Every maze is generated randomly when you open the page or press New Maze, so each puzzle is different.
