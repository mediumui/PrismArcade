About Jump Runner

Jump Runner is a free online endless runner that runs in the browser with no download or account. Press Space or tap the canvas to jump over the spikes that scroll toward you. The longer you survive, the faster the run gets. Your best distance is saved on this device for instant replay value.

Can I play Jump Runner online for free?

Yes. Jump Runner is free to play in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do I control Jump Runner?

Press the Space bar or tap the canvas to jump. Time each jump to clear the spikes as they scroll toward you.

What is the goal of Jump Runner?

Survive as long as possible and build the most distance. One collision ends the run, and your best is saved locally.
