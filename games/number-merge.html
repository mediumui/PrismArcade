About Number Merge

Number Merge is a free online merge puzzle that runs in the browser with no download or account. Tap one tile, then tap an equal tile to combine them into the next number. Every merge adds points equal to the new tile value. The goal is to build the highest tile and the biggest score before the board fills up.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Number Merge on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Tap one tile, then tap an equal-value tile to merge them.

  • 3. Plan your taps to build the biggest combined total.
