Merge Rules

Tap one tile, then tap an equal tile. If the values match, they combine into the next number. Your goal is to build the highest tile and the biggest score.

  • Only equal tiles can be merged.
  • Every merge adds points equal to the new tile value.
  • Works with mouse, touch, or keyboard-friendly taps.
