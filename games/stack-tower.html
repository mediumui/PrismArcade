About Stack Tower

Stack Tower is a free one-tap arcade game that runs in the browser with no download or account. A block slides above your tower; tap or click to drop it, and any part that overhangs the layer below gets sliced away. Perfect drops rebuild your width, so a steady rhythm beats frantic tapping. Each round is a quick two- to three-minute session built for high-score chasing.

How to Play

Quick steps to start Stack Tower on Prism Arcade.

  • 1. Watch the block slide back and forth above your tower.
  • 2. Tap, click, or press Space to drop it onto the tower.
  • 3. Line it up cleanly so no part overhangs and gets sliced away.
  • 4. Five clean drops in a row rebuild your width — chase the tallest tower.
