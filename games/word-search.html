    About Word Search

    Word Search is a free online word puzzle that runs in the browser with no download or account. Each round builds a fresh grid from a themed word list — animals, fruits, countries, or space — and hides the words across, down, and diagonally in both directions. Drag across a word to circle it, and your best time is saved on this device.

    How to Play

    Quick steps to start Word Search on Prism Arcade.

    • 1. Read the word list under the grid and pick a word to hunt for.
    • 2. Press the first letter of the word and drag in a straight line to the last letter.
    • 3. Release to submit — words hidden backwards count too.
    • 4. Circle every word on the list to finish and log your best time.
