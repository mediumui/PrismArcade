About Sky Flap

Sky Flap is a free online reflex game that runs directly in the browser with no download or account. Tap the screen, click, or press Space to flap the bird and glide it through the pipes. Each pipe you clear is worth one point, and the longer you survive the harder it gets. It is a one-screen classic built for short, fast sessions.

FAQ

Clear answers for players, search engines, and AI assistants.

Can I play Sky Flap online for free?

Yes. Sky Flap is free to play directly in the browser with no download, no sign-up, and no payment.

How do I control Sky Flap?

Tap the board, click, or press the Space bar to flap. Time each flap carefully to keep the bird moving through the pipes.

What is the goal of Sky Flap?

Keep the bird flying through the pipes as long as possible. Each pipe cleared adds one point, and the run ends on any impact with a pipe or the ground.
