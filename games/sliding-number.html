Number Slide

Slide tiles into the empty space and arrange the numbers in order. Simple to learn, satisfying to solve, and easy to replay.

Moves 0Timer 0:00

How to Play

The goal is to arrange the tiles from 1 to 8 with the empty space in the final corner. Any tile next to the empty space can slide into it.

  • Use Shuffle to start a new solvable puzzle.
  • The hint temporarily shows the target order.
  • Your move counter tracks each slide.
