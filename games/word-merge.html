How to Play

A pool of letters appears. Tap them to type a word, then merge it. Correct words grow your bin and your score; a wrong word costs a little. Every letter can be used once per level.

  • Tap a letter to add it to the input.
  • Tap it again to remove it.
  • Merge a valid English word to score.
  • Clear the whole pool to advance.

About Word Merge

Word Merge is a free online word puzzle that runs in the browser with no download or account. Tap letters to build real English words, then merge them into the bin for bonus points. Clear the letter pool to level up. It is a one-screen brain teaser that combines vocabulary with quick decision-making.

Quick steps to start Word Merge on Prism Arcade.
