Contact Us

Questions, bugs, or ideas? We read every message. The fastest way to reach the team is by email or the form below.

Reach us directly

Email: support@prismarcade.top

We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. Please check your spam folder if you don't hear back.

What can you contact us about?

  • Bug reports — a game won't load, score doesn't save, or controls feel wrong. Tell us the game name, your device, browser, and what happened.
  • Feedback & ideas — tell us which games you love or what you'd like us to build next.
  • Game requests — suggest a classic or original game you'd like added to the arcade.
  • Copyright & DMCA — if you believe content on the site infringes your rights, email us with the details and we will review and act promptly.
  • Partnerships & press — collaboration or media inquiries are welcome.

Before you write

Most answers are already on the About page and the Privacy Policy. A quick look there may save you a wait.

What happens next

When you email us, your message goes straight to the people who maintain the arcade. We keep a light inbox and answer in the order messages arrive. If your note is about a specific game, please mention the game's name and the device or browser you were using so we can reproduce the issue quickly. We never add your address to a mailing list, and we will not share it with third parties.

Send a message

This form opens your email app with your message pre-filled. If nothing happens, just email support@prismarcade.top directly.
