Privacy Policy

Prism Arcade is a free, static web arcade. We are committed to keeping your visit private and easy to understand.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who we are

Prism Arcade ("we", "us", or "the site") is a lightweight browser game collection. The entire site is served as static files with no account system, no login wall, and no paid features. You can open any game and play immediately.

2. Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask you to submit personal information such as your name, email address, or postal address in order to play. We do not operate a comment system, a forum, or any user registration.

Like almost every website, your browser automatically sends basic technical information when it requests a page. This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the date and time of the request. This information is used only to deliver the page correctly and to understand aggregate traffic; it is not used to identify you personally.

3. Cookies and advertising

To keep the site free, Prism Arcade may display third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. These cookies are set and controlled by Google, not by us.

You can disable the DART cookie and manage personalized advertising at any time by visiting the Google Ads Settings page, or by opting out of third-party ad cookies through YourAdChoices. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's advertising consent framework handles consent before non-essential ad cookies are set.

We also use a small amount of local storage in your own browser to remember game progress, high scores, or interface preferences. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

4. Analytics

We do not run analytics scripts that build a profile of individual visitors. Any traffic understanding is limited to anonymous, aggregate counts that cannot be traced back to you.

5. Children's privacy

Prism Arcade is designed for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under the age of 13. Our games are family-friendly, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because we collect essentially no personal information to begin with, there is usually nothing to export or erase. You can still reach us to ask, and we will respond within the time frames required by law.

7. Data retention

Because we do not store personal accounts or submitted data, there is no personal profile retained on our side. Server logs that contain IP addresses are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for security and are not used for advertising.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, please write to us at support@prismarcade.top or use the Contact page. We aim to reply within a few business days.
